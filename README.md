# Profile #

*I am 217763, L/Cpl Asumadu Sekyi Shirley. I was born on 22 March 2001 and hail from Ejisu-Onwe in the Ashanti Region. I am the third of six siblings. My father, Nana Asumadu Sakyi, is a sub-chief of Amoafo-Bekwai and my mother, Madam Gifty Asumadu Sakyi, is a farmer*.
## Education
I began my elementary education at Bethel Presby Experimental School, Atonsu-Agogo. I completed Junior High School at Adumasa All Saints Presby JHS in 2017.

*I then attended Kofi Agyei Senior High School, located at Fawoade-Mamponteng, where I studied General Arts and completed my secondary education in 2020. Afterward, I applied to study nursing and gained admission to Mampong Nursing and Midwifery Training College in 2021*.

## Military Career
My career path took a different turn and I applied to join the Ghana Armed Forces. I was recruited on 27 May 2022, at the age of 22.

*After completing six months of Basic Infantry Training Course 2-22 (BITC 2-22) at the Jungle Warfare School, Achiase, I passed out on 11 November 2022. I was then posted to the 64 Infantry Regiment, Gondar Barracks, Burma Camp, where my infantry journey began*.

In 2024, I attended the Clerk Course. After completing the course, I was posted from the 64 Infantry Regiment to Headquarters, Army Special Operations Brigade (HQ ASOB), where I currently serve as a Clerk GD.

## Current Training and Interests
I am currently enrolled in the Basic Information Technology Course for Non-Commissioned Officers 2-26, as part of my commitment to learning and advancing my personal and professional development.

I consider myself willing to learn, grow and experience new environments. In my leisure time, I enjoy singing, having fun and taking part in educational and recreational activities.
